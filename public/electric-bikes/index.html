Electric bicycle questions

Is an eBike the same as an electric bicycle?

Yes. eBike, e-bike and electric bicycle are common names for a bicycle with an electric motor that assists the rider while pedalling.

Where can I test ride a Pogon electric bike?

Free test rides can be booked in Belgrade, Novi Sad, Kragujevac and Niš, Monday to Saturday from 09:00 to 18:00. The Pogon team confirms the exact appointment and location.

Does Pogon deliver electric bikes across Serbia?

Yes. Courier delivery is available throughout Serbia. In-stock models are normally delivered within one to three business days.
